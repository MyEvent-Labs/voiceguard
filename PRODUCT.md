# PRODUCT.md — MyEvent Labs VoiceGuard

## What it is
A real-time speech-enhancement audio plugin (VST3 / AU / standalone) for live
events. It makes lectern, lavalier, handheld, headset, panel, webinar and
broadcast speech cleaner, clearer, more consistent and safer, with no added
latency.

## Who uses it, and where
Live sound engineers and broadcast/AV operators, at a front-of-house desk or a
control room, often mid-show, one screen, low ambient light, under time
pressure. That scene — not category habit — is why the interface is dark and
meter-forward.

## The job
Insert VoiceGuard, press AUTO, mix the show. The four macros (CLEAN, CLARITY,
LEVEL, PROTECT) are the primary controls; the advanced panel exists for the
engineer who wants it and stays out of the way otherwise.

## Success looks like
- The engineer can see at a glance what the plugin is doing (input/output
  level, gain reduction per stage, speech activity, protection state).
- A change is audible and legible without reading a manual.
- Nothing moves or decorates while someone is reading levels mid-show.

## Product truth (uninventable)
- 100% local; no network, account, telemetry or transcription.
- 0 added algorithm latency, lookahead-free; reported honestly.
- Alpha, unsigned; not for show-critical use.
- JUCE 8 / C++20 desktop; not a web app.

## Assumptions (labelled — no interview ran)
- "Modern and clean" = restrained dark neutral, one accent, strong type
  hierarchy, generous spacing, no skeuomorphism.
- Macro-first, meter-forward layout is preferred over a knob wall.
- The four macros and per-stage gain-reduction readouts are the hero.

## Mode
Operate — the visitor completes a task; precision and restraint outrank
expression.
